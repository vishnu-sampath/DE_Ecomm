# products

## Description
Contains master data for products sold by the business.

## Grain
One row per product.

## Business Use Cases
- Product catalog management
- Category-level analysis
- Cost and margin calculations

## Columns
- product_id: Unique identifier for the product
- product_name: Name of the product
- category: Product category
- supplier_id: Identifier of the supplier
- cost_price: Current or average cost of the product
